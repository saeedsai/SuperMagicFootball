# v0.8.11.2 — Game balance and defense hotfix

- Expanded field in both dimensions, with all formation spawn coordinates mapped to new pitch.
- AI secondary defender presses more consistently; off-ball allies shift toward danger.
- Held PASS initiates and maintains ball chase; automatic normal tackle when arriving in reach.
- Held JUMP without possession triggers defensive sprint, and PASS+JUMP performs fast pursuit.
- Running fatigue rate increased from 0.010 to 0.015 per second at full intensity (before endurance multiplier).
- Super-shot cost raised from 0.045 to 0.085, combo super from 0.060 to 0.105 (before endurance multiplier).
- Retains super-shot transformation timeout at 1.65 seconds, returning the loose ball to ordinary appearance.
- Updated one defensive-ability test to isolate its out-of-range scenario on the larger field.
