## Delivered

- `MatchOpportunityTracker` links real `keeperDiveAttempt` and `keeperContact` events to the current active ordinary/Super Shot and the opposite defensive team. Early visual activity is **not** counted as an actual save or an intervention.
- Per-shot optional `keeperDive` diagnostic records: attempted/contacted, goalkeeper ID, short-horizon predicted seconds, lateral offset on attempt, actual X-contact depth and actual lateral offset. Missing/nonfinite measurements remain absent rather than inventing a value.
- `keeperCalibration` in the `report()` payload: total shots; attempted, contacted, paired; attempts without contact; contacts without an early attempt; unlinked events; mirrored by defending team and by ordinary/Super Shot. Repeated frame events for the same shot count once in shot denominators.
- Four focused test contracts, plus a full-regression CI trigger on changes to the tracker, test, or report. This diagnostic sidecar does not attach to a public scoreboard and has no gameplay effect.

## Interpretation and outstanding gaps

A `keeperDiveAttempt` is a *visible predicted intent*, not a physical catch. A `keeperContact` means actual on-plane interception; its event is not itself a guaranteed catch/save outcome. Unlinked events intentionally remain separately counted. This avoids false inflated keeper-save rates and differentiates ordinary from Super Shots. No actual seeded shot **rate samples** are generated by the new four synthetic attribution cases; that is the next active requirement. Existing gameplay browser tests already exercise early intent and no phantom saves at 30/60/120Hz in both directions.

Five follow-ups: (1) mirrored seeded live keeper outcome telemetry and conservative movement calibration, (2) eight-direction glove-grip and distribution art, (3) pass/first-touch animation, (4) provenance-aware concurrent data merges, (5) career and tactics usability. Far-future physical-device/PWA/persistent-restart certification and multiplayer are deferred by the owner.
