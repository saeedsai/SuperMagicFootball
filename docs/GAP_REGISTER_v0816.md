# Open-Gap Register — v0.8.16

## Closed or improved in this milestone

- **Closed (base mechanic):** five attacking super-shot levels, level-4/5 named-star rarity and strength scaling.
- **Closed (base mechanic):** five independent defensive-super levels and rare goalkeeper-special tiers, including enhanced force/reach and lower stamina costs.
- **Closed (base mechanic):** inverse super-fatigue scaling across levels; low-speed transformed shots are not forced to become fast at higher levels.

## Immediate gaps (not yet complete)

1. **Move library:** 30–40 distinct supershots, 20 unique defensive specials and 10 rare GK moves remain planned; current historical/remake move catalog is limited.
2. **Super level growth:** levels can be edited or assigned as defaults, but actual skill XP/training/upgrades and unlock criteria are not built yet.
3. **Goalkeeper behaviors:** catch/throw/long-punt feedback, collision animations and smarter distribution remain partial.
4. **AI active defending:** designated pressers and cover exist but collision-aware positioning and full strategy are incomplete; long-session balance tests are pending.
5. **Mobile controls:** an Android flying-kick QA first pass was intermittent under load; isolated rerun passed. Real touchscreen and controller acceptance tests are not complete.
6. **Character authenticity:** most of 2,592 players use procedural caricatures; full hand-reviewed likenesses, 20 full face silhouettes and 20 hair templates remain pending.
7. **Roster authenticity:** 1,776 fictional/academy placeholders were still present in v0.8.13 baseline; actual 2026 club membership, transfer values and positions need continued audits.
8. **Competition expansion:** World Cup, continental national cups, at least 48 national teams and the seven BPW teams remain in the roadmap.
9. **Career/online:** manager depth, full player growth, improved save/cloud workflows, online multiplayer and accessibility remain incomplete.
10. **Hardware/browser coverage:** physical Android/iOS, Bluetooth gamepads, Firefox/Safari, long-session and GPU testing remain unverified.

The full 216-row source of truth is `FEATURE_MATRIX_216_ITEMS.csv`; earlier audit CSVs, reports and roadmaps remain in the source release.
