# Supermagic Football v0.8.14 — Appearance Expansion Phase 1

- Added **20 unique eye-shape presets** and **20 unique eyebrow presets** to the cartoon character renderer.
- Added **6 eye colors**, **6 nose shapes** and **8 lip shapes** with deterministic generation and per-player customization support.
- Added **body build**, **stature** and **funny-scale** controls so avatars can become more exaggerated, comic and visually diverse.
- Expanded signature appearance presets for major football stars/legends to better match their stylized in-game identity.
- Improved player graphics and kits with stronger collar/trim treatment, shorts/socks detailing, highlights and more expressive chibi proportions.
- Updated the Player Customization screen to expose the new appearance controls and randomization coverage.
- Added/updated automated tests covering unique facial-feature counts, deterministic appearance ranges and renderer stability.
- Rebuilt the standalone offline `play.html` package and bumped exported/save-facing version metadata to **0.8.14**.

## Notes

- This is a **stylized cartoon likeness** pass, not a photoreal or licensed likeness pipeline.
- The roster-structure corrections from v0.8.13 remain preserved and continue to pass regression.
- A larger curated manual identity pass across priority clubs/nations is planned for **v0.8.15**.
