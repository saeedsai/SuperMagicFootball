# Open-Gap Register — v0.8.15

## Closed or improved in this milestone

- **Improved:** Active defensive teammate and CPU opponent pressing. Designated pressers exclude human-controlled players, update per frame and attempt tackles inside reach.
- **Improved:** Goalkeeper short/long distribution with explicit controls, plus automatic AI decision based on pressure and distance.
- **Closed for base special-shot path:** Jumping transformed shots and teammate aerial super shots are launched at a reachable height with descending vertical momentum.

## Immediate gaps (not yet complete)

1. **Five-level attacking super ability:** need exact tier scale, stamina relief, level assignment and rarity validation for levels 4 (very few stars) and 5 (superstars).
2. **Five-level defensive super ability:** power, stun, counters, rarity and fatigue relief as above.
3. **Smarter football AI overall:** current pressing is a bounded improvement, not an expert tactical engine; needs longitudinal match balancing, goalkeeping choices, support runs and collision/pathfinding work.
4. **Advanced GK behaviors:** explicit manual catching/throwing, goalkeeper positional commands and deterministic cross-device multi-touch testing.
5. **Appearance refinement:** most 2,592 players still procedurally rendered; manually curated elite cartoon faces and full 155–210cm editor pending.
6. **Roster authenticity:** 1,776 placeholder slots remained after v0.8.13; player club membership, transfer values, ratings and physical features not comprehensively verified.
7. **Super-move catalog:** 30–40 distinct shots, 20 defensive moves, 10 keeper specials; different speed/path/impact, animation and historically inspired behaviors.
8. **National & BPW tournaments:** national-team expansion, continental cups, World Cup and seven era teams with no duplicate canonical BPW players.
9. **Physical QA:** Android/iOS hardware, real gamepads, Safari/Firefox, accessibility, long session balancing and online play.

Full list of 216 items remains in `FEATURE_MATRIX_216_ITEMS.csv`. Existing roadmap docs and prior audits have not been discarded.
