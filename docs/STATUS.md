## 16 October 2026 — proactive goalkeeper dive attempts and full-scope roadmap audit

The active modular engine now records `keeperDiveAttempt` and starts a directional goalkeeper dive/recovery animation as an incoming shot approaches the physical save plane. It reads an already existing deterministic short-horizon projection of credible on-target shot lanes and guards against high/outgoing/far-wide shots. A 1.25-second attempt gate prevents frame-by-frame repeat events. The keeper **does not move to a predicted ball coordinate**, the ball is not accelerated/claimed by the intent, and the original contact-depth check still decides all actual saves. This is a small visible goalkeeper-activity improvement, **not** a claim that genuine keeper interception/save percentage is higher.
