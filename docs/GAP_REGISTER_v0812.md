2. **Contextual goalkeeper polish:** distribution, catches, throws, manual keeper switching, and keeper-specific collision/reaction behavior still need stronger integrated tests.
3. **Roster accuracy:** 2026 rosters have generated player placeholders, incorrect positions/attributes; the three audit CSVs provide leads, not automatically verified truth.
4. **Creator/likeness:** 20 face/20 hair/10 facial hair catalog, body height/types and recognizable world-class faces remain unfinished.
5. **Moves/physics:** historic original mechanics, free kicks/corners/cards and roughly 40+20+10 distinct attack/defence/GK supernatural abilities remain incomplete.
6. **Tournaments:** 48+ modern national teams, World Cup, national continental competitions and seven BPW teams not yet implemented.
7. **Career/network:** online multiplayer, large domestic seasons and richer transfers remain prototypes or missing.
8. **QA caveat:** the historical browser scripts include timing-sensitive mobile cases and some old hard-coded version assertions; regression status requires actual green reruns.

The original v0.8.11.2 larger pitch/defensive sprint/fatigue and v0.8.11.1 super-shot return-to-normal fixes are retained.
