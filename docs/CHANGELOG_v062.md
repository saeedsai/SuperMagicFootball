# v0.6.2 Alpha — 2026-10-13

One bounded release focused on individual attributes; no work on future flag/crest systems, online, new national clubs or morale in this release.

- New per-player stat schema and deterministic migration of existing 216 x 12 roster slots.
- Added per-user/CPU fatigue, fatigue performance multipliers, and non-resetting kickoff with partial halftime recovery.
- Changed tackling to aggression/defence vs strength contests and linked knockdown duration to outcome.
- Wired numerical attributes into speed, shots/aim, headers, passes/crosses, GK reach, handling and penalties.
- Full values plus active ability names in Squad screen, selected player stamina HUD and career stat readout. Career training now increases position-relevant new 1–100 attributes.
- Added 14 requirements to the tracked backlog; split advanced super abilities into a future dedicated milestone.
- New Node and desktop/mobile browser QA; old test suites retained.

Important limitations: numerical ratings for historical squads are design estimates, not verified attributes. Some goalkeeper and aerial abilities are only partial; per-player defense super ability and rare GK super ability metadata exists but activation and unique animations are not done.
